ORBILINK Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ORBILINK ("we", "our", or "the application") collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you use the ORBILINK messaging application and associated services accessible via https://orbilink.github.io/.

We are committed to user privacy and transparent data practices. ORBILINK is designed to provide secure real-time messaging, audio/video communication, and productivity integrations while minimizing data collection.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with ORBILINK, we may collect the following categories of information:

Account Information

  • Email Address: Used for account creation, identification, password recovery, and service communications.
  • Account Identifiers: A unique system-generated user identifier (UID) associated with your authenticated session.

Username & Profile Information

  • Username: A unique handle chosen by you to identify your account and allow other users to discover and message you.
  • Display Name: The name shown to other participants in your chats and channels.
  • Avatar & Status: Profile photos and custom status messages you choose to upload or configure.

Messages and Chat Data

  • Chat Content: Text messages, conversation metadata (timestamps, sender identity, message status, read receipts, and reactions) transmitted between conversation participants.
  • Group & Channel Data: Group titles, descriptions, and participant membership rosters.

Uploaded Media and Files

  • Media Files: Voice recordings (audio notes), images, and documents you actively choose to attach and send within conversations.

Authentication Information

  • Credentials: Account passwords are not stored in plain text. Authentication is handled using secure, industry-standard authentication mechanisms (including Firebase Authentication) utilizing salted, cryptographic hashes.

Google Account & Workspace Data (Explicit Consent Only)

ORBILINK only accesses Google user data when you explicitly grant permission through an interactive Google OAuth consent screen. If you do not connect your Google account, no Google data is accessed. When authorized:

  • Google Contacts Data: Accessed only if you grant Google Contacts / People API access. We use this exclusively to display your address book within the application and correlate contacts with other registered ORBILINK users so you can connect with them.
  • Google Drive Data: Accessed only if you grant Google Drive API access. We use this strictly to let you browse your Drive documents and select files to share or attach within your chat conversations.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for the following functional purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain real-time messaging, audio notes, and media exchange.
  • To authenticate your identity and protect against unauthorized access.
  • To display contact presence (online status and last active timestamp) to conversation partners.
  • To enable optional address book correlation and Google Drive file attachments when requested by you.
  • To diagnose technical issues, prevent abuse, and uphold application reliability.

4. Google API User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

Google API Compliance: ORBILINK's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • No Sale of Google User Data: We do never sell Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks under any circumstances.
  • No Advertising: Google user data is never used, transferred, or disclosed for serving advertisements or targeted marketing.
  • No Unauthorized AI Model Training: Google user data accessed via Google APIs is not used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Minimal Data Retention: Google OAuth tokens and synced contact or file references are used transiently to serve your requests in real time.

5. Revoking Google Permissions

You have full control over third-party Google permissions and may revoke access at any time:

  • In-App: You can disconnect your Google account through the ORBILINK Settings or Contacts/Drive view, which removes active session tokens.
  • Via Google Security Settings: You can permanently revoke ORBILINK's access to your Google Account at any time by visiting Google Account Third-Party Apps & Services.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain user data only for as long as necessary to provide the service:

  • Message & Media Deletion: Users can delete individual messages or media within conversations.
  • Account Deletion: You may request complete deletion of your ORBILINK account, associated profile, and conversation records at any time by contacting our support team. Upon verification, your account and associated database records will be permanently removed.

7. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your data:

  • All network communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Database and storage access is enforced through strict security rules ensuring that only authorized participants can access specific conversation messages and files.
